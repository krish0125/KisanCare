PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year transferred entirely directly into the bank accounts of farmer families across the country in three equal installments.

  • Eligibility: Landholding farmers
  • Benefit: ₹2000 every 4 months
  • Deadline: Open all year (eKYC mandatory)

Full Protection

Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

A comprehensive crop insurance scheme to provide financial support to farmers suffering crop loss or damage arising out of unforeseen events.

  • Premium: 1.5% - 2% (Rabi/Kharif)
  • Coverage: Drought, Flood, Hailstorm
  • Claim: Direct settlement to bank

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Provides farmers with timely access to credit for agricultural expenses like seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and other inputs at highly subsidized interest rates.

  • Interest Rate: 4% (with prompt repayment)
  • Limit: Up to ₹3 Lakhs collateral free
  • Extras: Includes animal husbandry & fishery

Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization

Get massive financial assistance / subsidies on buying new tractors, rotavators, tillers, and modern farming equipment.

  • Benefit: 50% to 80% Cost Covered
  • Target: Small & Marginal Farmers
  • Process: Apply via State Agri Portals

₹50,000 / ha

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas (PKVY)

Promotes organic farming through a cluster approach. Government provides financial assistance to farmers shifting to chemical-free agriculture.

  • Funding: ₹50,000 per hectare for 3 years
  • Usage: For organic seeds, bio-fertilizers
  • Market: Help with organic certification

"Per Drop More Crop" - Subsidies on implementing micro-irrigation systems like Drip and Sprinkler irrigation in your fields to save water.

  • Subsidy: 55% for small/marginal farmers
  • Goal: Water conservation & yield boost
  • Apply: District Horticulture Office
