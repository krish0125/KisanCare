store Fertilizer Store & Usage Guide

Compare Solid vs Liquid fertilizers, understand how to apply them, and purchase verified products directly.

grain Solid Fertilizers

What is it? Traditional Granules or powder forms (e.g., Urea, DAP, MOP).

Which is Best for: Foundational soil preparation before sowing and long-term, slow release of nutrients over weeks.

Step-by-Step Application:

  1. Measure: Calculate the exact bag weight needed per acre.
  2. Prepare Soil: Ensure the soil has slight moisture. Do not apply on dry, cracked soil.
  3. Method 1 (Broadcasting): Walk linearly and scatter the granules evenly by hand across the field.
  4. Method 2 (Band Placement): Use a seed drill to place the fertilizer 5cm away and 5cm below the seed line.
  5. Irrigate: Lightly water the field within 24 hours to help the granules mix and prevent nitrogen loss.

water_drop Liquid / Nano Fertilizers

What is it? Concentrated liquid formulas (e.g., Nano Urea, Liquid NPK).

Which is Best for: Instant nutrient absorption during rapid growth, lowering costs, and preventing soil/water pollution.

Step-by-Step Application:

  1. Dilute: Mix 2-4 ml of liquid fertilizer per 1 litre of clean water in your sprayer tank.
  2. Timing: Pick a clear morning (before 10 AM) or evening (after 4 PM) to avoid evaporation. Don't spray if rain is expected.
  3. Method 1 (Foliar Spray): Pump the sprayer and coat the plant leaves thoroughly until wet.
  4. Method 2 (Drip Fertigation): Mix the liquid load directly into your drip irrigation system's venturi tank.
  5. Safety: Wear gloves and a mask. Do not mix incompatible pesticides.

Verdict: Which one is the Best?

For the best yield, combine both! Use Solid Fertilizers (DAP/NPK) at the time of sowing (Basal Application) to give the seeds a strong foundation in the soil. Later, during the vegetative and flowering stages, switch to Liquid Spray Fertilizers (Nano Urea/Liquid NPK) for Top-Dressing. Liquid is ultimately superior for the environment and highly cost-effective, but solids are still mandatory for initial root establishment.
